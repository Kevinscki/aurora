The Vaquita

The rarest mammal on Earth

Introduction

The vaquita is an extremely small, short-snouted porpoise endemic to the upper Gulf of California (Sea of Cortez) in Mexico. It has been classified as Critically Endangered by the IUCN, and currently represents the rarest mammal on Earth.

Threats

The primary threat to the vaquita is illegal fishing for totoaba, a species of whalefish whose fat contains an endangered pharmaceutical drug called saponin. The use of large, non-selective gillnets in totoaba fisheries has resulted in massive bycatch of vaquitas.

Population ~10 Individuals
Status Critically Endangered
Range Gulf of California

Conservation Efforts

International organizations, including the World Wildlife Fund (WWF), are working to protect the vaquita. A no-fishing zone has been established in its habitat, and anti-poaching patrols are being deployed.

Every effort is being made to ensure this unique species survives.
