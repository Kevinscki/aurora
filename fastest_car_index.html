Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+

Defying the Speed Limit of Reality

The Legend Reborn

The Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+ is not just a car; it is a statement. Based on the legendary Chiron, this hypercar was engineered to shatter the previous speed record and push the boundaries of what an eight-cylinder W16 engine can achieve.

With its aerodynamic masterpiece design and advanced engineering, it stands as the undisputed champion of production cars in terms of raw velocity.

Key Specifications

440 mph Top Speed
8.0s 0-60 mph
1,578 hp Power Output
1,600 lb-ft Torque

Engineering Marvel

The heart of this machine is an 8.0-liter W16 quad-turbocharged engine that delivers a staggering 1,578 horsepower and 1,600 lb-ft of torque. This unprecedented power-to-weight ratio allows the Chiron Super Sport 300+ to accelerate from 0 to 62 mph in just 2.4 seconds.

Aerodynamics play a crucial role here. The car features a rear wing that deploys at speeds above 186 mph, creating significant downforce to maintain stability at these extreme velocities.

The Record

In September 2019, the Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+ set a new world record for the fastest production car, reaching a top speed of 304.775 mph (490.48 km/h). It remains a testament to human engineering and the pursuit of perfection.
